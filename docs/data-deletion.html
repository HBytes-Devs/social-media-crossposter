Data Deletion Instructions

Social Media Crossposter (SMC) — maintained by HBytes Devs

Last updated: July 2026
Contact: haseebcodejourney@gmail.com

How to request deletion of your data

If you connected SMC to Facebook or another platform and want your data removed, use one of the options below.

Option 1 — Delete your SMC account (recommended)

  1. Log in to Social Media Crossposter.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account, or contact support if that option is not yet available.
  4. We delete your user record, connected platform tokens, posts, and uploaded media.

Option 2 — Disconnect a platform only

  1. Log in to SMC.
  2. Open Accounts or Settings → Connected accounts.
  3. Disconnect Facebook or any other platform.
  4. We remove that platform's stored tokens and linked page/profile identifiers.

Option 3 — Email request

Email haseebcodejourney@gmail.com from the address tied to your SMC account with the subject Data deletion request. Include:

  • Your registered email address
  • Which platform connection(s) to remove (e.g. Facebook)
  • Whether you want full account deletion or only platform disconnect

We process verified requests within 30 days.

What we delete

  • User profile (email, name)
  • OAuth access tokens for connected platforms
  • Scheduled and published post records stored in SMC
  • Media files you uploaded to SMC storage

Facebook users

If you authorized SMC via Facebook Login, you can also remove the app under Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites, then request deletion using the steps above so we remove any data we still hold for your account.
