## 1. Overview

**Social Media Crossposter (SMC)** is an all-in-one marketing dashboard. Publish content across social channels and **view performance in one place** — without switching between Google, LinkedIn, and Meta dashboards.

### Why SMC for analytics?

| Benefit | What it means for you |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Single panel** | Ads and post performance in one branded dashboard |
| **Secure connection** | Official platform login (OAuth) — SMC never stores your passwords |
| **Read-only reporting** | View metrics safely; campaign changes stay in Google/LinkedIn Ads Manager |
| **Date flexibility** | Last 7, 30, or 90 days — or pick a custom range |
| **Self-hosted option** | Your data stays on your infrastructure (enterprise deployments) |

### What SMC is — and is not

| SMC **is** | SMC **is not** |
|------------|----------------|
| A unified view of ads and content performance | A replacement for Google Ads Editor or LinkedIn Campaign Manager |
| A cross-posting + reporting hub | A tool that changes bids, budgets, or ad creative |
| Built for marketers who want clarity | A third-party analytics SaaS with extra fees per seat |

## 4. Google Ads in SMC

### What you get

Connect your **Google Ads account** and see:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Impressions** | How often your ads were shown |
| **Clicks** | Visits driven by your ads |
| **Cost** | Total ad spend for the selected period |
| **Conversions** | Actions completed on your site (when tracked) |
| **CTR** | Click-through rate — ad relevance indicator |

**Views available:**

- Account summary for your chosen date range  
- Daily trend charts (clicks, cost)  
- Campaign breakdown table — compare campaigns side by side  

### How to connect (3 steps)

1. Open **Google Ads** in the SMC sidebar  
2. Click **Connect Google Ads** and sign in with your Google account  
3. Click **Sync now** to refresh the latest data  

### What you can control from SMC

| Action | Supported |
|--------|-----------|
| Connect / disconnect your Google Ads account | ✅ Yes |
| View metrics and charts | ✅ Yes |
| Refresh data (Sync) | ✅ Yes |
| Create or edit campaigns | ❌ No — use [Google Ads](https://ads.google.com) |
| Change budgets or bids | ❌ No |

> **Note:** Google requires an approved developer connection for live ad accounts. Your administrator completes a one-time platform setup; after that, you simply connect and sync.

## 5. LinkedIn in SMC

LinkedIn is supported in **two ways** — organic content and paid ads.

### 5A. LinkedIn posts (organic content)

**Publish** thought leadership, updates, and images to LinkedIn from SMC, then **measure** how they perform.

| Metric | What it tells you |
|--------|-------------------|
| Impressions | How many times your post was seen |
| Members reached | Unique professionals who saw it |
| Reactions | Likes and similar engagement |
| Comments | Conversation on your post |
| Reshares | How often others shared your content |

**Where to view:** Dashboard → LinkedIn performance panel, or **Posts** → view stats on individual posts.

**Requirement:** LinkedIn must approve analytics access for your organization’s app. Until then, publishing works; detailed stats may be limited.

### 5B. LinkedIn Ads (paid campaigns)

Same experience as Google Ads — connect your **LinkedIn Ads account** and monitor:

- Impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, CTR  
- Account totals and campaign-level breakdown  
- Charts for the last 7, 30, or 90 days  

**How to connect:**

1. Open **LinkedIn Ads** in the sidebar  
2. **Connect LinkedIn Ads** → approve access  
3. **Sync now**  

**Control from SMC:** View and sync only. Campaign management stays in [LinkedIn Campaign Manager](https://www.linkedin.com/campaignmanager/).

## 9. Security & Data Privacy

Your trust matters. SMC is built with enterprise-grade practices:

- **Official platform login** — You authorize SMC through Google, LinkedIn, or Meta; passwords are never stored  
- **Encrypted credentials** — Connection tokens are encrypted at rest  
- **Minimum access** — SMC requests only the permissions needed for publishing and reporting  
- **You stay in control** — Disconnect any platform account at any time from SMC  
- **Read-only ads data** — SMC reports on performance; it does not modify your live campaigns  

Platform partners (Google, LinkedIn, Meta) may require their own app review for production analytics access. Your SMC administrator handles this once; end users simply connect their accounts.
