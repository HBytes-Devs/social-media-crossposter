# Privacy Policy — Social Media Crossposter (SMC)

**Last updated:** July 2026  
**Contact:** [haseebcodejourney@gmail.com](mailto:haseebcodejourney@gmail.com)

## Overview

Social Media Crossposter ("SMC", "we", "our") is operated by HBytes Devs. This policy describes what data we collect when you use SMC and how we use it.

## Data we collect

- **Account information:** email address and display name when you register.
- **Connected platform data:** OAuth tokens and profile/page identifiers for platforms you connect (e.g. LinkedIn, Facebook, Reddit).
- **Content you create:** post text, images, and scheduling preferences you submit through SMC.
- **Usage data:** basic logs needed to operate the service (errors, API requests).

## How we use data

- Authenticate you and connect your social accounts.
- Publish and schedule posts on your behalf to platforms you authorize.
- Provide analytics and billing features where enabled.
- Improve reliability and security of the application.

## Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

- **Social platforms** you connect (to publish on your behalf).
- **Infrastructure providers** required to run SMC (hosting, database, storage).
- **Payment processor** (Stripe) if you subscribe to a paid plan.

## Data retention

We retain your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove associated tokens, posts, and stored media in line with our data deletion process.

## Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us at the email above or following the [Data Deletion Instructions](DATA_DELETION.md).

## Security

We store OAuth tokens encrypted and follow industry-standard practices to protect your information.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in this document with an updated date.
